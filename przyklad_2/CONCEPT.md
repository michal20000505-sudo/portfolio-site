# SENTRACKER / TRC-01

## Campaign idea

**THE TRAIL WRITES BACK.**

SENTRACKER does not sell speed as an abstract promise. The campaign treats the
TRC-01 outsole as a reader: separate lugs map broken ground, split cushioning
times heel strike against toe-off, and the acid sidewall line connects impact to
release. The page moves from contact mapping into surface change and material
under load.

## Product

`TRC-01 / FIELD SIGNAL`

An original low-profile trail-fashion sneaker with an asymmetric plum ripstop
upper, irregular graphite quick-lacing, a smoky TPU heel frame and a segmented
acid-chartreuse outsole. A single yellow line runs through the construction as a
visual pressure path. This is a fictional concept product; construction labels
describe design intent, not laboratory results.

## Visual system

- **Environment:** near-black plum `#160B22` and dark plum `#2A1043`.
- **Signal:** acid chartreuse `#B6FF00` and sharp yellow `#F1FF4B`.
- **Evidence:** paper-like off-white `#F3F0E8`.
- **Anchor:** graphite `#111111`.
- **Typography:** Barlow Condensed for force and scale, Cormorant Garamond italic
  for a controlled editorial interruption, system mono for coordinates.
- **Grid:** a 12-column editorial field with visible registration lines and
  asymmetric image crops.
- **Mark:** a geometric coordinate frame and pressure trace joined to a condensed
  SENTRACKER wordmark, never a location pin or footprint symbol.

## Experience

1. **Calibration:** a short pressure-scan loader; no decorative percentage.
2. **Suspension:** the product hangs inside monumental campaign typography.
3. **Trajectory:** a sticky scroll sequence turns one yellow trace into motion.
4. **Construction:** the outsole rotates onto an off-white technical sheet while
   the product layers separate.
5. **Terrain shift:** a single boundary frame moves from wet asphalt into gravel.
6. **Material under load:** macro crops show where ripstop yields and TPU holds.
7. **Colorway index:** three independent material renders share one product stage.
8. **Registration:** a typographic final statement marks the study for the session.

## Motion rules

- Product movement is heavy and inertial.
- Signal lines are fast, precise and linear.
- Display type enters in clipped, decisive cuts.
- Photography reveals more slowly through masks.
- Pointer interaction is limited to the hero and selected controls.
- Touch devices remove hover behavior.
- Reduced-motion mode removes the loader wait, parallax, scrubbed rotation and
  smooth scrolling while keeping the complete static composition.
