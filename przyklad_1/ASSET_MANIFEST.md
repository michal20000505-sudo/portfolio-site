# Generated asset manifest

All three final campaign images were generated with the built-in image
generation tool, then converted locally to optimized WebP at quality 88.

The supplied Pinterest board was used only as a broad mood reference for
editorial confidence, analog print character and compositional energy. No
specific work, poster, typography, person or layout was copied.

## `public/images/fault-hero.webp`

Original fashion-editorial photograph for a fictional exhibition about
pressure, memory and terrain: an androgynous adult performer in a long worn
vermilion coat, caught by wind in a pale limestone quarry. Vertical crop,
expansive negative space, real 35mm slide-film scan character, chalk / bone /
graphite palette, no embedded text, logos or glossy CGI finish.

## `public/images/fault-contact.webp`

Campaign detail in the same world: one natural dusted hand pressed to a
tool-marked limestone cut, red cotton entering the wide frame as a graphic
plane. Radical asymmetry, material detail, restrained light leak, no text,
logos, jewelry or surreal anatomy.

## `public/images/fault-passage.webp`

Same anonymous performer and coat from a high oblique viewpoint in a narrow
quarry passage. Severe landscape geometry, quiet negative space, aged 35mm
European fashion-editorial texture, no collage decoration, extra people or
cinematic orange–teal grading.
