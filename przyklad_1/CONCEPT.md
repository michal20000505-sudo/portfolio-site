# FAULT / 08 — concept

## Premise

`FAULT / 08` is a fictional field archive examining how pressure leaves a
record in bodies, cloth and mineral surfaces. It behaves like the digital
edition of an uncompromising small-run art journal rather than a conventional
studio or product landing page.

## Visual language

The composition borrows a shared grammar from experimental editorial design:
overscaled condensed type, tiny technical notation, abrupt changes of scale,
severe crops, large zones of quiet material and controlled collisions between
image and text. It does not reproduce any individual reference layout.

The recurring graphic device is a red *pressure register*: a bar, rule or plane
that cuts through each section and changes function as the visitor moves. The
desktop archive turns horizontal for one chapter, echoing the act of examining
contact sheets. On mobile it becomes a paced vertical sequence with different
type breaks and image crops.

## Palette and type

- limestone / paper: `#e8e4da`
- ink: `#171714`
- signal vermilion: `#da2d1f`
- mineral gray: `#a9a69f`
- Barlow Condensed: labels, display scale, hard rhythm
- Cormorant Garamond: reflective statements and editorial counterpoint

Monospace is reserved for coordinates and measurements.

## Photography

Three original images form one campaign: the same anonymous performer, one worn
vermilion coat, a pale limestone quarry and cool overcast daylight. The
photographs are intentionally tactile—dust, fabric wear, film grain, imperfect
exposure—and avoid sterile rendering or generic cinematic grading.

## Motion

The page opens by assembling the masthead as pieces of a printed forme. Scroll
then changes crop, direction and scale rather than applying one repeated
fade-up. Motion never carries essential meaning, and the reduced-motion version
retains the complete hierarchy without pinning or smooth scrolling.
